Bali Clothing · Rest-of-year plan · 27 Oct – 31 Dec 2026 · v2

Q4 growth plan: get more people to the site

October shows the site is converting better than last year. Fewer people are reaching it. This plan rebuilds traffic and the email list through Black Friday and Christmas while paid spend stays low. It leans on Sinéad, O'Reilly's, creators, email and organic content.

Conversion is up. Traffic and list growth are down.Sessions fell 30% and new email sign-ups fell from about 540 a month last Nov–Dec to 89 this October. Each extra 1,000 sessions is worth about €1,300 in net sales at October's rate.

What it means

  • The ad cut explains most of the fall. Facebook traffic halved. Branded Google search fell 24% too, because fewer people are seeing Bali in their feeds.
  • Acquisition is the problem, not loyalty. Returning customers held steady and new customers more than halved.
  • Social traffic converts at 0.8%. Direct and search convert at 2.1–2.3%. Creator and partner content sends warmer visitors than brand ads do.

Email: the unengaged test is done

  • October's sends deliberately included the unengaged 180-day segment, to see who is really gone after a year of patchy availability and launches. Open rates of 23–27% and €0.04–0.06 per recipient give that answer.
  • Engaged-only sends last year earned €0.10–0.32 per recipient. The next step is a sunset flow (section 05), then sending to engaged subscribers by default.
  • Flows are short of new people: the welcome series reached 216 people in October. Fixing the sign-up form and running lead-gen campaigns fixes that.

04 · The strategy

Five moves

1

Sinéad as the face of Bali this Christmas

Content from a real person outperforms brand content at low spend. Sinéad's posts can serve as organic content, partnership ads and email at the same time.

  • Weekly collab posts: 2 Reels or TikToks a week co-posted with Bali (they appear on both grids), plus Stories most days. Formats: "what I'm wearing this week", try-ons in two sizes, gift box packing and Black Friday picks.
  • Partnership ads: Sinéad grants ad permission on Instagram and shares a TikTok Spark Ad code once. Bali then boosts the best post each week from Sinéad's handle instead of making separate ads.
  • Lead-gen host: Sinéad fronts the Gifting Edit giveaway (section 05), with entries going into Klaviyo.
  • Emails from Sinéad: plain-text notes between designed sends, such as "what I'd buy on Black Friday" or "the last day for Christmas delivery". A personal sender usually lifts opens in peak weeks.
  • Named code (for example SINEAD_BALI) so sales are counted.
2

O'Reilly's: list the edit, collaborate and share audiences

Last year O'Reilly's traffic converted at 3.4%, better than any other social source. O'Reilly's drew 58,188 sessions in four weeks in May–June 2026, about ten times Bali's monthly traffic.

  • List a Bali edit on O'Reilly's (live w/c 9 Nov): 6–8 products, all with depth: Signature Oversized Hoodie, Signature Half Zip, Sculpt Leggings, Sculpt Full Zip, Baseline Oversized Hoodie, plus Beanie, Socks and gift boxes. Keep prices the same as baliclothing.ie. Agree the model (wholesale, consignment or drop-ship), stock and returns by 6 Nov.
  • Collab posts from both accounts at launch, again at Black Friday and for the December gift guide.
  • Partnership ads from the O'Reilly's handle, aimed at O'Reilly's followers and lookalikes.
  • Joint giveaway in w/c 16 Nov, with entrants opting in to each brand separately.
  • A slot in O'Reilly's email at launch and in BF week, with UTM-tagged links. Use "as stocked at O'Reilly's" in Bali's own content.
3

Launch the Gifting Edit, not a new product

  • The Grounded drop is live (26 Oct). The next launch is the Gifting Edit on Mon 16 Nov: gift boxes and bundles built from in-stock heroes, such as the hoodie and leggings set, the half zip and beanie, and the "cosy box" of hoodie, socks and beanie.
  • The Winter Beanie is the gift with purchase over a set spend from BF week, which lifts order value without a deeper discount.
  • Push gift cards and e-gift cards from 14 Dec, once courier cut-offs pass.
  • The men's hoodie is parked. It sold 29 units in 14 months, so it doesn't earn a launch slot in peak season.
4

Email: sunset the unengaged, grow the list, cover December

  • Build the sunset flow now (section 05) so the list is clean before peak.
  • Send to engagers (30/60/90 days) plus all purchasers by default. Send 2 a week in November and daily in BF/Cyber week, then cover December properly.
  • Run lead-gen campaigns and update the sign-up form (section 05) to get sign-ups back toward 500 a month.
  • Check that campaign links carry UTMs. Shopify logged only 60 email sessions in October against roughly 800 Klaviyo clicks.
5

Grow organic content fast

The aim is volume without extra production, so batch content, reuse it and recycle anything that works (section 06).

05 · Email build

Sunset flow, lead gen and the form

Sunset flow

Entry: a segment of people subscribed for over 90 days with no click in 180 days, no site visit in 90 days and no order in 365 days. Use clicks rather than opens, because Apple Mail opens inflate open rates. Exit as soon as someone clicks, orders or visits the site.

Day 0
"Still want to hear from us?"Short and honest: "We've been quiet and some sizes sold out. Here's what's back." Show the in-stock heroes and three preference buttons: Keep me in, Only new drops and sales, Unsubscribe. Add a line about Black Friday early access to give them a reason to stay.
Wait 4 days
Split: clicked or ordered → exit back to the main list. Otherwise continue.
Day 4
"Something to come back for"One incentive, such as free shipping or a beanie with any order, valid for 7 days. Use a product grid of best sellers with stock.
Wait 5 days
Split: engaged → exit. Otherwise continue.
Day 9
"This is our last email (unless you say otherwise)"Plain text from Sinéad, with one button: "Keep me on the list."
Wait 3 days
Still nothing: add the profile to a "Sunset – suppressed" list and suppress from email marketing. Keep their SMS consent if they have it.

Timing: run the first batch from 2 Nov so it finishes before the 16 Nov gift launch. People who haven't responded can still get the BF day and Cyber Monday emails. Final suppression happens on 7 Jan, after peak, when lost sales are least likely. After that, the flow runs all the time for anyone who goes quiet.

Sign-up form update

What to change

  • Run one pop-up at a time. Bali Club Signup and Grounded Popup are both live, so they compete. Set clear rules or merge them.
  • Offer from 2 Nov: "Black Friday early access + 10% off your first order". Switch on 1 Dec to "Gift guide + Christmas delivery dates".
  • Two steps: email first, then an optional phone number on step two. This rebuilds SMS for BF.
  • Timing: show on the second page view or at 40% scroll on mobile, and on exit intent on desktop. Hide it on checkout, cart and from existing subscribers. Keep a small teaser tab after it's closed.
  • Mobile first: one product image, a short headline and a single field.

Benchmarks

  • Q4 2025 benchmark: 6.5% submit rate on Bali Club Signup. Target 5%+ from November.
  • Sign-ups to aim for: 500 or more a month in Nov and Dec (last year: 509 and 569).
  • Keep the drafts tidy: there are 20+ draft forms. Archive the old ones so the live setup is clear.

These are specs only. I didn't change any form or flow in Klaviyo.

06 · Organic content

How to grow it fast

Make more from less

  • One half-day shoot a week produces 15–20 short clips: try-ons, gift box packing, outfit swaps, detail shots and talking to camera.
  • Reuse everything: each clip goes to TikTok, then Reels, Stories, Facebook and email, and the winners become ads.
  • Repost creator and Sinéad videos on Bali's channels (collab or stitch) so other people's content fills the calendar.
  • Customer content: add a "tag us and you could be featured" ask to the existing Customer Thank You flow, and repost the results.

Cadence and series

  • November: 1 TikTok a day, 3 feed posts a week and daily Stories. BF week: 2 TikToks a day.
  • Repeat series people come back for: "Sizing Sunday" (the same piece in two sizes), "Pack orders with us", "One hoodie, three ways" and "What Sinéad's wearing".
  • Reply to comments with video to answer fit and sizing questions; it also creates content.
  • Every Monday: pick the top 3 posts by saves, shares and site clicks. Boost them and make more like them.
