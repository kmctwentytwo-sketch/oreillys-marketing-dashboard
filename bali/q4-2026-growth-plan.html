Q4 growth plan: €100k online

This combines Steven's Online Sales Plan (targets, dates, product and kids plan, ad checks) with the marketing plan. The top priority is organic content: daily, consistent and pushed harder than any other part of the plan, because that's what brings traffic back.

The website converts. We need traffic, and content is the cheapest way to get it.Conversion and order value are holding. Daily sessions dropped from about 240 to about 100 from 15 Oct. €100k needs roughly 490 sessions a day on average and about 850 a day in the Black Friday window.

02 · What October told us

Traffic fell from mid-October; the website held

Shopify, 1–26 Oct vs last year

  • Sessions down 30% (6,629 → 4,665). Conversion up (1.52% → 1.65%) and add-to-cart rate up (6.4% → 7.9%).
  • New customers down 55% (75 → 34). Returning customers held steady (54 → 55).
  • Facebook sessions halved (1,907 → 966). Instagram fell 26% and Google search fell 24%.
  • Sales were 20% ahead from 1–14 Oct, then 58% behind from 15 Oct (Steven).

The ads are reaching a similar number of people but sending fewer of them to the site. Before adding budget, check: was spend front-loaded before 15 Oct, is the campaign objective set to sales, how do clicks and CTR compare with last year, is creative fatigued, and is the Meta pixel still recording purchases? Fresh organic and creator content fixes the fatigue problem too.

Email context: October's sends deliberately included the unengaged 180-day segment, to see who was really gone after a year of patchy availability and launches. The result (open rates of 23–27% and €0.04–0.06 per recipient) gives the answer, so the next step is the sunset flow in section 07.

04 · Priority one

Organic content, every day

Posting every day isn't too much at this time of year. People shop more and scroll more, and Black Friday and gifting give a reason to post. Content is also the free fix for tired ad creative and the cheapest route back to 490 sessions a day. The rule is every day, no gaps, from 27 Oct to 31 Dec.

1–2TikToks a day (2 a day from 19 Nov to 2 Dec), each cross-posted as a Reel
5+Feed posts a week on Instagram and Facebook (carousels, product, Sinéad)
DailyStories, 5–8 frames: polls, countdowns, new in, restocks, reposts

How to keep up the volume

  • Two capture sessions a week: one half-day product shoot (15–20 clips) and one phone-shot session for BTS, Sinéad and team content.
  • Build a 30-clip bank by 15 Nov so Black Friday week is posting, not filming.
  • Schedule a week ahead in Buffer. Leave one live slot a day for trends and replies.
  • Reuse every clip: TikTok, then Reels, Stories, Facebook, email GIFs and, for winners, ads.
  • Use other people's content: Sinéad, creators, O'Reilly's and customers. Add a "tag us" ask to the Customer Thank You flow.
  • One owner for the calendar, so a busy day never means no post.

How to measure it

  • Every Monday: pick the top 3 posts by saves, shares and link clicks. Make more like them and boost them as ads.
  • Track posts per channel against plan, views, profile visits, link clicks and social sessions in Shopify.
  • Target: social sessions back to last year's level (about 2,960 a month in October) by mid-November.
  • Reply to comments with video. It answers fit questions and gives you the next post.

06 · Product and offers (Steven's plan)

What to push and how to price it

Adults

  • Protect full price. Keep the average discount at or below 27%; every 5 points is worth about €6,000. Keep deep discounts to the BF window. Outside it, add a free beanie or socks over a spend threshold.
  • Lead with the heroes: Baseline Oversized Hoodie (23% of last year's sales), Origin Leggings with Pockets and half zips. Watch hoodie sizes before BF.
  • Gift Box is the Christmas hero from BF to the shipping cut-off. Target 350–400 (about 300 last year). Reserve hoodie, fleece and half zip stock for it.
  • Socks and beanies made up 27% of items but 6.5% of sales. Use them in bundles, Gift Boxes and gift with purchase.
  • No ad money for low-volume lines (Men's Baseline Hoodie, Ribbed Leggings).
  • Gift cards are the main offer once the delivery cut-off passes.

Kids: €12,000 target

  • 890 units in stock (700 hoodies at €35, 90 fleeces, 100 new joggers). Hitting €12k means selling about 350 kids hoodies.
  • Kids Gift Box alongside the adult one, from BF to the cut-off.
  • Mini-me bundles: matching adult and kids hoodies and half zips. This is strong content too.
  • Kids in every gift guide, Christmas email and homepage section from mid-November.
  • €1,000–1,500 of the Nov–Dec ad budget goes to parents, scaled only if it's producing purchases.

07 · Email

Sunset, lead gen, the form and the send plan

Sunset flow

Entry: people subscribed for over 90 days with no click in 180 days, no site visit in 90 days and no order in 365 days. Use clicks rather than opens, because Apple Mail opens inflate open rates. Exit as soon as someone clicks, orders or visits the site.

Day 0
"Still want to hear from us?""We've been quiet and some sizes sold out. Here's what's new." Show Grounded and the heroes, three preference buttons (all emails, drops and sales only, unsubscribe) and the BF early-access hook.
Wait 4 days
Clicked or ordered → exit back to the main list.
Day 4
"Something to come back for"A free beanie or socks with any order for 7 days. This protects full price, as in Steven's plan.
Wait 5 days
Engaged → exit.
Day 9
"This is our last email (unless you say otherwise)"Plain text from Sinéad, with one button: "Keep me on the list."
Wait 3 days
No response: add to a "Sunset – suppressed" list. They get only the BF day and Cyber Monday emails, then are suppressed on 7 Jan.

Lead-gen campaigns

  • BF early access list (27 Oct – 18 Nov): pop-up, link in bio, Meta lead ads and Stories. It pays off on Thu 19 Nov.
  • Sinéad gift box giveaway (9–15 Nov): Klaviyo landing page, boosted as a collab post and partnership ad.
  • O'Reilly's joint giveaway (16–22 Nov), with separate opt-ins for each brand.
  • Drop waitlists and back in stock: the back-in-stock form converts at 53%. Turn on "notify me" for Drop 2 and Drop 3 as soon as they have dates.
  • Gift guide sign-up (from 12 Nov), including kids.

Sign-up form

  • Bali Club Signup converted at 6.5% last Q4 (1,676 sign-ups) and 2.3% in Sep–Oct this year. Sign-ups fell from about 540 a month to 89.
  • One pop-up live at a time. Bali Club Signup and Grounded Popup are both live now.
  • Offer: "Black Friday early access" now, then "Gift guide + Christmas delivery dates" from 1 Dec.
  • Two steps: email, then optional phone number to rebuild SMS. Show on the second page view or at 40% scroll on mobile, exit intent on desktop. Hide on checkout and from subscribers.
  • Target a 5%+ submit rate and 500 sign-ups a month.

10 · Weekly rhythm

Every Monday

  • Compare last week against the phase target (sales, orders, sessions, kids) and agree what changes.
  • Review content: posts published against plan, the top 3 posts, and what to remake and boost.
  • Log every email, ad change, post spike and promotion in the Note column of the daily report, so you know what drives each spike.
  • If a week finishes more than 20% behind target, act that week: add an email, push more content or increase spend on ads that are converting.
  • Update the drop placeholders as soon as dates are confirmed.
