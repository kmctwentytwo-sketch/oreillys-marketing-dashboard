Bali Clothing · Rest-of-year plan · 27 Oct – 31 Dec 2026

Q4 growth plan: get more people to the site

October shows the site is converting better than last year. Fewer people are reaching it. This plan uses email, a product launch, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, creators and partnership ads to rebuild traffic while paid spend stays low. It runs through Black Friday and Christmas.

Conversion is up. Traffic is down 30%.Sessions fell from 6,629 to 4,665 while conversion rose from 1.52% to 1.65%. Each extra 1,000 sessions is worth about €1,300 in net sales at October's rate, so the job for the next nine weeks is traffic.

What it means

  • The ad cut explains most of the fall. Facebook traffic halved. Branded Google search fell 24% too, because fewer people are seeing Bali in their feeds.
  • Acquisition is the problem, not loyalty. Returning customers held steady and new customers more than halved.
  • Social traffic converts at 0.8%. Direct and search convert at 2.1–2.3%. Social content needs to send warmer visitors, which creator and partnership content does better than brand ads.

Email (Klaviyo, October)

  • Campaigns went to about 10,300 people, including the "Unengaged 180 days" segment. Open rates were 23–27% and revenue per recipient was €0.04–0.06.
  • Last October's launch email went to 5,500 engaged people. It opened at 42% and made €0.30 per recipient, about six times more.
  • The store-credit email to purchasers was this month's best performer: €0.35 per recipient and a 6.3% click rate.
  • Flows have too little traffic to work with. The welcome series reached 216 people and abandoned cart reached 110. They will grow as traffic returns.

What sold last Q4 and what is selling now

The Baseline Oversized Hoodie was last year's Q4 hero, with €18.1k and 373 units. Its men's cut, the Baseline Oversized Hoodie – Mens, sold 29 units (€1.4k) and is no longer in the range. Stocking fillers were big too: the Winter Beanie sold 309 units and Crew Socks sold 324. This October the leaders are the Signature Oversized Hoodie (€1.4k), Sculpt Leggings (€1.3k) and Signature Half Zip (€1.1k). The "Gift Boxes Drop" email was last November's best campaign, at €2.0k.

03 · The strategy

Five moves

1

Build a partnership-ads engine: Sinéad, Steven, O'Reilly's and creators

Run Bali ads from other people's handles: Meta partnership ads (Instagram and Facebook) and TikTok Spark Ads. Partnership ads usually get cheaper clicks and higher click-through than brand-handle ads because they look like recommendations. That's what a smaller budget needs.

  • Sinéad and Steven: post collab posts (shared to Bali's grid) and grant ad permission once, so the best organic posts can be boosted without new production. Use a talking-to-camera format: "what I'm wearing this winter", "the hoodie I live in", gift picks.
  • O'Reilly's: collab posts on the O'Reilly's Instagram and TikTok to launch the "Bali at O'Reilly's" edit (move 3). Run them as partnership ads aimed at O'Reilly's audience and lookalikes.
  • Gifted creators already in the Hub: Ciara Gillespie, Emma Murray, Kate Gallagher, Gerry Lavery, Amanda Woods and Georgia Keys have product. Next are the larger approved names, Niamh Gallagher (257k TikTok) and Cáoileánn Conway (237k TikTok). Gerry Lavery is the natural lead for the men's hoodie.
  • Fix the bottleneck: in the Influencer Hub, the "Whitelisted", "Content Live" and "Ad Live" columns are almost all unticked. Content is getting made but not used. Get permissions and TikTok ad codes in before Black Friday, and require commercial-safe audio, as the existing reminder template already asks.

Why: social traffic fell 41% and it converts at 0.8%. Creator content brings back reach and warmer clicks without returning to old spend levels.

2

Launch the men's hoodie as the Christmas product story

  • Bring back the Baseline Oversized Hoodie – Mens in 2–3 core colours. Position it as "the one for him", with matching His & Hers sets alongside the women's Signature and Baseline hoodies.
  • Waitlist first (2–11 Nov): run a "Notify me" sign-up from organic, creators and email. This builds the list for BF and shows how many units to commit.
  • Launch on Thu 12 Nov at full price. That's two weeks before Black Friday, so it's in hand for gifting and gets a full-price window. Include it in BF as a bundle (hoodie + beanie + socks) rather than a straight discount.
  • If stock can't land by 12 Nov, run it as a pre-order with a clear "ships by 10 Dec" date. A gifting product that arrives after the Christmas cut-off is a lost sale.

Why: it gives Bali new news to share while ad spend is low, it opens a second buyer (women buying for partners, brothers and dads), and it raises order value through sets.

3

List a Bali edit on O'Reilly's

  • O'Reilly's drew 58,188 sessions in four weeks in May–June 2026 (from the O'Reilly's dashboard in this repo). That's roughly ten times Bali's monthly traffic. Listing a curated edit puts Bali in front of shoppers it can't currently afford to reach.
  • Edit of 6–8 products: Signature Oversized Hoodie, Signature Half Zip, Sculpt Leggings, Baseline Oversized Hoodie (women's and men's), Crew Socks and Beanie as add-ons, plus gift boxes.
  • Keep prices the same as baliclothing.ie so there's no channel conflict. Agree the model (wholesale, consignment or drop-ship), stock allocation and returns in writing before 6 Nov.
  • Go live w/c 9 Nov with a collab post from both accounts, a slot in O'Reilly's email and a partnership ad from the O'Reilly's handle. Use UTM-tagged links back to Bali for brand content.

Why: it borrows a larger audience at near-zero media cost. It also gives Bali a credible "as stocked at O'Reilly's" line for its own social and email.

4

Rebuild email around engaged segments, then go big at Black Friday

  • Default send: 30/60/90-day engagers plus all purchasers, about 5–6k people. That's the audience that made €0.30 per recipient last year. Leave out the unengaged 180-day segment from routine sends.
  • Unengaged segment: send one "still want to hear from us?" email with the BF early-access hook in w/c 9 Nov, then include it only in BF and Cyber Monday sends. Sunset it after Christmas to protect deliverability.
  • Cadence: 2 sends a week through November, rising to a daily send in BF/Cyber week. Plain-text "note from Sinéad" style emails between designed sends do well in peak weeks.
  • Lead with purchasers: October's best email was store credit to purchasers. Repeat that mechanic as "VIP early access" 24 hours before Black Friday.
  • Capture: change the site pop-up offer to "Get Black Friday early access" from 2 Nov, and push the men's hoodie waitlist. Both feed the list before peak, when sign-ups are cheapest.
  • Check that campaign links carry UTMs. Shopify logged only 60 email sessions against roughly 800 Klaviyo clicks, so email is probably under-credited.

These are recommendations only. I didn't change anything in Klaviyo.

5

Run organic TikTok and Instagram as a weekly content system

  • Volume: 5 TikToks or Reels a week, 3 feed posts and daily Stories. Post short-form natively to TikTok and cross-post to Reels.
  • Formats that sell activewear: try-on and fit checks by size ("5'4", size S"), "one hoodie, three outfits", couples and His & Hers styling, unboxing gift boxes, behind the scenes of packing BF orders, and stitching creator videos.
  • Collab posts by default: use the Instagram collab tag on every creator, Sinéad, Steven and O'Reilly's post so it shows on Bali's grid as well.
  • Facebook: it's still Bali's biggest social traffic source (966 sessions). Use it for the gift guide, the Christmas cut-off countdown and partnership ads aimed at gift buyers aged 35+.
  • Use the best organic performers each week as the paid creative. Don't make separate ad content.
