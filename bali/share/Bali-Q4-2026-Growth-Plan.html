03 · Drops and key dates

Plan every drop as a 15-day window

Drops have been planned around the launch day, so the team ends up reacting. From now on each drop gets 7 days of build-up, the launch, and 7 days of follow-up. That way every piece of content gets used fully and as many people as possible see the product before and after it lands. Production has to start three weeks out, so the teaser content exists before the build-up starts.

How to keep up the volume

  • Two capture sessions a week: one half-day product shoot (15–20 clips) and one phone-shot session for BTS, Sinéad and team content.
  • Build a 30-clip bank by 15 Nov so Black Friday week is posting, not filming.
  • Schedule a week ahead in Buffer. Leave one live slot a day for trends and replies.
  • Reuse every clip: TikTok, then Reels, Stories, Facebook, email GIFs and, for winners, ads.
  • Use other people's content: Sinéad, creators, O'Reilly's and customers. Add a "tag us" ask to the Customer Thank You flow.
  • One owner for the calendar, so a busy day never means no post.

How to measure it

  • Every Monday: pick the top 3 posts by saves, shares and link clicks. Make more like them and boost them as ads.
  • Track posts per channel against plan, views, profile visits, link clicks and social sessions in Shopify.
  • Target: social sessions back to last year's level (about 2,960 a month in October) by mid-November.
  • Reply to comments with video. It answers fit questions and gives you the next post.
05 · Creative

Hooks, captions, CTAs and testing

More spend can't fix weak ads, and choosing what looks nice isn't a test. Every post and ad starts from a planned hook and CTA, gets tested, and is kept or dropped based on its numbers. Brand rules for type, tone and sign-off are in the Bali brand guidelines.

Caption formula

  1. Hook line (repeat the on-screen hook)
  2. One useful detail: fit, fabric, size worn, price
  3. Proof: a review line, "sold out last year", stock level
  4. One CTA
  5. 3–5 hashtags (#activewearireland #donegal plus the product)

Example: "Leggings that don't roll down, squat-tested. Sculpt Leggings, high waist, size S on 5'6. '4th pair, never buying anything else' (Judge.me review). Shop Sculpt via the link in bio. #activewearireland #leggings"

CTAs by stage

  • Get seen: "Follow for Thursday's drop" · "Save this for your Christmas list" · "Send this to whoever you're buying for"
  • Consider: "Comment SIZE and we'll help you pick" · "Size guide in bio"
  • Buy: "Shop the Grounded drop, link in bio" · "Get Black Friday early access, link in bio" · "Order by [date] or collect in Ballybofey"
  • One CTA per post, and match it to the phase in the calendar.

Testing: what to measure and when to decide

Organic (every week)Paid (every week)
TestOne variable a week. Usually 3 hooks on the same footage, posted Mon, Wed and Fri. Then test captions, length and CTAs.A small testing campaign of 3–5 new ads with equal budgets, taken from each phase's existing budget (about 15%), not new money.

Reuse winning creative

  • Bali's own past winners: pull the top ads from Nov–Dec 2025, when ads returned €17.27 per €1 in November. Re-cut and re-run the best before making anything new.
  • Competitors' winners: in the Meta Ad Library, an ad that has run for 30+ days is usually profitable. Copy the format and hook, not the content.
  • TikTok Creative Center: top ads and trending sounds for activewear in Ireland and the UK.
  • Creator posts that outperform go straight into partnership ads.

Winners library

Add a "Winners" tab to the Influencer Hub. Each entry: a link, the hook, the format, the key number (hook rate, CTR, sales), why it worked and the Bali version to make. Every Monday, pick 2 to remake that week. Nothing gets made without a reason from this list or a planned test.

Sunset flow

Entry: people subscribed for over 90 days with no click in 180 days, no site visit in 90 days and no order in 365 days. Use clicks rather than opens, because Apple Mail opens inflate open rates. Exit as soon as someone clicks, orders or visits the site.

Sign-up form

  • Two steps: email, then optional phone number to rebuild SMS. Show on the second page view or at 40% scroll on mobile, exit intent on desktop. Hide on checkout and from subscribers.
  • Target a 5%+ submit rate and 500 sign-ups a month.
