Brand guidelines

How Bali looks, sounds and gets signed off, so daily content can move fast and stay consistent. The fonts and colours come from the live Shopify theme. The tone of voice starts from Klaviyo's analysis of past Bali emails.

Draft for Sinéad's sign-off. The brand line and tone pillars below are proposals. Everything else documents what's already in use.

01 · Brand

What Bali is

Activewear for real life in the north-west: training, school runs and everything in between. Owner-led from Ballybofey.

Who it's for

Women (and their families, through the kids range) who train, walk and live in their activewear and want it to fit properly. Mostly in Ireland and Northern Ireland.

What sets it apart

The owner is visible, the team is local, and fit is taken seriously. Bali shows how pieces fit real bodies, and customers can collect in Ballybofey.

02 · Logo

The BALI wordmark

BALIIllustration only. Always use the master files.
  • Files: BALI-BLACK-Bold (on light backgrounds) and BALI-WHITE-Bold (on dark backgrounds and photos). Both are in Shopify Files.
  • Clear space: keep at least the height of the "B" free around the logo.
  • Don't stretch, recolour, outline, add shadows or put it on busy parts of an image.
  • Minimum size: 18px tall on mobile screens (the site uses 18px mobile and 27px desktop).

Website and email

  • The website uses Inter: 700 for headings, 500 for subheadings, 400 for body. Buttons are uppercase with square corners.
  • Email uses Helvetica today. Switch it to Inter, with Helvetica and Arial as fallbacks, so email matches the site wherever web fonts load.
  • Use sentence case for headings. Save uppercase for buttons and short labels.

Social on-screen text

  • Use Inter Bold in Canva or CapCut. In the TikTok app, use the "Classic" font, the closest native match.
  • Keep it to 8 words or fewer per line, in sentence case, black or white with no outline.
  • Keep text inside the safe zone: clear of the top 15% and bottom 25% of a vertical video.
  • Turn on captions (subtitles) on every talking video.
05 · Tone of voice

Like the owner talking, not a sale sign

Klaviyo's analysis of past Bali emails describes the current voice as excited, urgent, inviting and persuasive, with frequent exclamation marks, capitals, emojis and "SHOP NOW". Keep the warmth and the directness. Use less urgency: people tune out urgency when it's on every email, which is part of why the unengaged segment grew.

Real

Talk like Sinéad or someone on the team would. Be honest about fit ("size up if you're between sizes") and about what went wrong ("we sold out too fast, it's back").

Local and proud

Ballybofey, Donegal and the north-west are part of the story. Packed here, collected here, worn here.

Encouraging

Fitness for everyone, at every size and stage. No "beach body", no guilt and no before-and-after.

Clear

Every message gives the size, the price, the date and what to do next. Short sentences. Talk to "you"; "we" means Sinéad and the team.

06 · Photo and video

Show the fit

  • Real people and real places: the team, customers and creators, in Donegal locations, gyms and at home. Use natural light.
  • Show the fit: front, side, back and sitting or squatting, with height and size on screen or in the caption.
  • On TikTok, a native look beats a polished one. Use the studio shoot for the website, email and the first frames of ads. Phone footage is fine for most social.
  • Use commercial-safe music on anything that might become an ad, including creator content.
  • Keep it neutral: clean backgrounds, so the product colour carries the image.
07 · Sign-off

Who approves what

Daily posting can't wait in a queue, so the level of sign-off depends on the risk. Most content is checked by the person posting it against the checklist below.

1

Daily organic: Stories, everyday posts, comment replies

The content owner checks it against the checklist and posts it. No other sign-off. Same day.

2

Campaign content: drops, emails, SMS, ads, creator briefs, giveaways

The marketing lead signs off. Steven checks every price, offer and date against the Online Sales Plan. Submit by launch −8 days. Turnaround 24 hours.

3

Brand-level: new campaign ideas, events, O'Reilly's collabs, discounts over 27%, new brand assets

Sinéad signs off. Turnaround 48 hours.

Checklist before anything goes out

  • Price, offer and dates match the sales plan
  • The product and sizes shown are in stock
  • Logo, Inter type and the neutral palette are used correctly
  • Tone rules are followed (urgency only when true)
  • Model height and size are stated
  • The hook is in the first 2 seconds and on screen
  • One clear CTA with a UTM-tagged link
  • Captions are on and alt text is written
  • Music is commercial-safe if it might be used as an ad
  • Creator posts carry #ad or #gifted, as Irish (ASAI) and UK (ASA) rules require
  • Giveaways have terms and a clear opt-in
  • It's logged in the content calendar
